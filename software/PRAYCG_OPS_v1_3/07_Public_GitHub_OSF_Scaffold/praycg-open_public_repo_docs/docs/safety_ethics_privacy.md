# Safety, ethics, and privacy

## Human-subjects review

This repository is not IRB approval. Any study involving other participants should receive appropriate ethical review or formal determination according to local requirements.

## Informed consent

Participants must understand:

- what sensors are used;
- what kinds of media may be shown;
- approximate session duration;
- what data are recorded;
- how data will be stored and shared;
- that participation is voluntary;
- how to stop the session;
- what, if anything, is withheld until debrief.

## Incomplete disclosure

If a study withholds exact hypotheses, condition labels, or the hypothesis-critical day, treat it as authorized incomplete disclosure, not casual deception.

Requirements should include:

```text
minimal risk;
scientific justification;
no feasible non-deceptive alternative;
prior consent to incomplete disclosure;
full debrief;
data withdrawal pathway where appropriate.
```

## Privacy

Do not publish:

- raw identifiable participant data;
- private names without permission;
- home location details;
- EXIF GPS metadata;
- private emails;
- private file paths;
- faces or voice recordings without explicit consent.

## Data sharing levels

Recommended public sharing ladder:

```text
Level 0: no human data - synthetic demo only.
Level 1: deidentified feature tables only.
Level 2: deidentified XDF with explicit consent and removal of unnecessary identifiers.
Level 3: controlled access for sensitive data.
```

## Media copyright

Do not upload copyrighted stimulus videos or audio. Upload scripts, manifests, hashes, QC procedures, and instructions for lawful user-supplied media.

## Hardware safety

The Box is not a certified shielded room or medical device. Maintain:

- ventilation;
- emergency exit;
- external access;
- fire safety;
- trip-hazard control;
- heat monitoring;
- electrical safety;
- safe seating and sensor cable routing.

Do not modify mains wiring. Do not attach experimental grounds to mains unless reviewed by a qualified electrician or relevant professional.

## Psychological safety

Narrative stimuli can be emotionally evocative. Screen for risk where appropriate, warn participants about content, allow stopping, and debrief respectfully.

## Analysis ethics

Negative or weak results should not be rescued by rhetoric. Exploratory findings must stay exploratory unless they are repeated under frozen analysis rules.
