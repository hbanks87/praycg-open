## Interpretation

NUPI is a secondary/exploratory profile layer. It is designed to sit beside TTI and NIP, under A-MRED as the primary endpoint. It estimates whether a story has an accommodative-load, resolutive-recovery, high-load-with-recovery, or unresolved recognition profile.

## Boundary

NUPI does not measure literal thermodynamic energy, consciousness, clinical restoration, or moral value. It classifies available PR-AYC-G proxy evidence.
