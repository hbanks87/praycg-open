## EEG and signal-processing disclaimer

Scalp EEG, especially gamma-band activity, is artifact-sensitive. Muscle activity, eye movements, jaw tension, electrode movement, line noise, respiration, posture, stimulus timing, and device behavior can produce false signals. No single metric in this repository should be interpreted alone.

It is not a certified shielded room, medical device, therapeutic device, or safety-certified enclosure. Do not modify mains wiring. Do not connect unsafe grounds. ## ALS-PT19 timing disclaimer

The ALS-PT19 / photodiode-style timing channel validates physical display timing. It belongs to the external input vector `u(t)`. It is not a biological photonic measurement and not a hidden variable `Y(t)`.

## Synthetic reproducibility disclaimer

Rung 0 synthetic programs verify analysis behavior in simulated data. They do not prove that the biological target exists. A synthetic radar can prove that it finds a simulated target; it does not prove a real target exists in a brain.
