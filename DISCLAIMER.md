# Disclaimer

PRAYCG is exploratory research software. It is not a medical device, clinical diagnostic tool, treatment system, lie detector, emotion detector, consciousness detector, or legal decision instrument.

The software can produce false positives and false negatives. EEG gamma is artifact-sensitive. Self-report contextualizes physiology but does not prove internal state. Synthetic Opto-PING results test mathematical identifiability only, not biological mechanism.

Do not use PRAYCG outputs for clinical, employment, educational, legal, relationship, or coercive decisions.
