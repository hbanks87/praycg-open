# Research ladders, rungs and prospective stages

PRAYCG uses several ladders for different questions. A synthetic rung, a human-study stage, a measurement gate and an evidence level are separate records. This index keeps every documented stage visible without treating a plan or a software result as completed biological validation.

## Opto-PING synthetic ladder: 0A-0O

The [Opto-PING index](../optoping/README.md#complete-documented-rung-0-ladder) covers the full documented sequence. The [everyday explanation v1.1](../studies/optoping-rung0/OptoPING_16_Point_Everyday_Explanation_v1_1_NullAware_LO.pdf), pages 4-7, describes 0A/0B easy recovery, 0C hidden-state recovery, 0D/0E/0F proxy robustness and 0G/0H/0I composite-K stabilization. Separate executable packages for those earlier stages were not located in the reviewed public tree or Opto-PING ZIP.

Rungs 0J-0O have preserved scripts, specifications, reports and outputs: positive lock-in, reproduction handoff, empty-sky specificity, null-aware selection, calibration repair and locked null-aware reproduction. L and M report partial/failed gates; their failures remain part of the record. These are synthetic sensitivity and specificity tests, not human or cellular mechanism measurements.

## Cross-scale evidence ladder

The [everyday explanation](../studies/optoping-rung0/OptoPING_16_Point_Everyday_Explanation_v1_1_NullAware_LO.pdf), page 4, separates the meanings of Y across scales. The [TSC priority stack v1.3](../measures/originals/master-suite-v1-3-kht/TSC_2026_PRAYCG_OSM_PRIORITY_STACK_v1_3.md) describes the progression as synthetic identifiability, human translation and later mechanism-specific cellular evidence. It does not assign a completed numbered rung to the later cellular work.

| Level | Defined object | Available record and status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematical definition | Y_math and the specified coupling equations | A model construct; see [Formula Report v1.0, section 38](../collections/public-docs/Formula_Module_Report_v1_0/PRAYCG_Formula_Module_Report_v1_0.md#38-opto-ping--rung-0-synthetic-model) |
| Rung 0 synthetic tests | Y_synth, whose truth is supplied by the generator | [0J-0O packages](../optoping/README.md#runnable-packages-locks-and-outputs); earlier stages documented in the handout |
| Rung 1 human translation | Inferred Y_HT and human-feature K_HT | [HumanTranslation v0.2](../measures/originals/master-suite-v1-3-kht/HUMAN_TRANSLATION_KHT_v0_2.md) and [module note](../measures/originals/master-suite-v1-3-kht/HUMAN_TRANSLATION_KHT_MODULE_v1_3.md); exploratory proxy modeling, not direct cellular observation |
| Human network-state development | K_HT-topo, event coupling and carryover | [CandidateLocal note](../measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/CANDIDATE_LOCAL_KHT_v1_4.md), [Topo-OSM reformulation](../measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/TOPO_OSM_NETWORK_STATE_REFORMULATION_v1_4_7.md) and [migration note](../measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/KHT_TO_KHT_TOPO_MIGRATION_v1_4_7.md); retains human-scale interpretation |
| Cellular/optical measurement | Y_cell and independently measured cellular coupling | Required future evidence; no completed cellular experiment or runnable cellular reproduction package identified in this indexed public collection |
| Mechanism-level OSM | Y_OSM / K_OSM | Reserved for mechanism-specific evidence; not established by synthetic success or the human proxy modules |

The [Topo-OSM claim boundary](../measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/TOPO_OSM_CLAIM_BOUNDARY_v1_4_7.md) separates human network-state interpretation from OSM-CELL. The same symbol or a similar fitted coefficient does not make evidence at these levels interchangeable.

## Seven-step inferential ladder

The [protocols, theory and analysis methods supplement](../protocols/originals/docs/PRAYCG_v0_93_to_v0_95_Protocols_Theory_and_Analysis_Methods.md#10-unified-inferential-logic), section 10, defines this sequence. These are questions a result must address, not seven already completed studies.

| Step | Question |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Manipulation | Did the intended stimulus/task state occur, supported by independent reports and quality checks? |
| 2. Measurement | Were timing, channels, coverage, artifact controls, cardiac/respiratory data and required features adequate? |
| 3. Within-run contrast | Did the frozen endpoint differ across the declared conditions? |
| 4. Alternative explanations | Do structural controls, sensory drive, respiration, task demand or artifacts weaken the attribution? |
| 5. Time structure | Does the effect exceed the declared timing, phase or label nulls where applicable? |
| 6. Prospective validity | Does the frozen candidate predict independent outcomes in new participants and stimuli? |
| 7. Reliability and calibration | Is the measure sufficiently stable and, where probability claims are intended, independently calibrated? |

The [separate Help-files occurrence](../protocols/originals/help-files/PRAYCG_v0_93_to_v0_95_Protocols_Theory_and_Analysis_Methods.md) remains available with its original provenance.

## Confirmation-grade gates

The [master theory and protocol v1.1](../theory/originals/docs/PRAYCG_Master_Theory_and_Protocol_PUBLIC_v1_1_v095.md), section 27, specifies seven gates: stimulus/media, anchors, timing, hardware, confounds, analysis and replication. Its replication gate requires repeated stimuli and sessions, participants, analysis without post-hoc tuning and ideally external operators. A passed installer, a finalized file or a synthetic reproduction does not by itself satisfy these combined requirements.

## Prospective human-study roadmap: Stages 0-3

Section 28 of the same [master theory and protocol](../theory/originals/docs/PRAYCG_Master_Theory_and_Protocol_PUBLIC_v1_1_v095.md) defines a separate numbered roadmap. Its Stage 0 is not Opto-PING Rung 0.

| Stage | Proposed work | How to read its status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Software/hardware reproducibility | Another user installs the Workbench, prepares media, checks streams, records XDF, produces analyses and understands quality/missingness reports | Reproduction objective; assess the exact release and operator evidence rather than assuming it is complete |
| 1. Individual replication | Repeated PRAYCG3/PRAYCG4 sessions with new stimuli, locked anchors, physical timing, confound reports and no post-hoc tuning | Proposed repeated-session work; exploratory self-runs are not independent group replication |
| 2. Small-N feasibility | Counterbalanced or partially counterbalanced sessions for usability, manipulation success and variance estimates | Feasibility objective, not confirmation |
| 3. Prospective group study | Preregistered endpoints, multiple stimuli, group counterbalancing, artifact/gaze controls, independent outcomes, explicit nulls and external reproduction | Prospective design requirements; this index supplies no new completion claim |
