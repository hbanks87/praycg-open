REDUCED RESEARCH CORE — working decision, 2026-11-08

The retained core is a small collection of bounded observations and reusable
methods. It is not a validated unified theory, a new scalar Presence formula or
an attempt to rename ordinary findings as framework-specific discoveries.

1. Keep the older-adult human-helper observation at its actual scope (REC04).
In the released task, the same 30 older participants were more accurate and
reported lower workload with human than robot assistance. Across assisted loads,
human minus robot was +2.0556 correct pairs and -4.3907 released NASA-TLX units;
the participant-average contrasts survived the specified Holm-nine adjustment.
The full sample comprised 60 people. This reanalysis uses the same people and
does not identify trust, metabolic energy, universal age moderation or a novel
offloading mechanism. The original numerical reconstruction was partial.

2. Keep the corrected model comparisons and their positive as well as negative
results (G05-G08). The Stroop decoder component improved over corrected ordinary
active inference by 0.018554 nats/trial, but an ordinary-attention alias tied it
and simpler lapse/frequency models predicted better. In the public bandit data,
ordinary active inference was competitive and outpredicted several RL/simple
models; the gate added only 0.000041 nats/choice and a Bayesian model without an
information bonus had the best point loss. These are task-specific comparisons,
not a general verdict against active inference, RL or adaptive learning.

3. Keep narrow mathematical/engineering results with their stated assumptions.
Some equations give valid restricted compression or useful implementation ideas.
Synthetic equivalences, counterexamples and positive controls remain informative
about those equations and designs. They are not human evidence. The nominated
efficiency gate saved counted operations but failed its joint accuracy/change
criteria; this was not a test of the separately installed application (G04). Selective ideal
interventions separated one synthetic causal alias pair (MP05); a feasible and
selective human intervention was not established. The fixed-metric meaning-empathy
pipeline passed its limited synthetic sensitivity/null qualification (MP14);
weighted cosine remains a candidate feature, not an established measurement of
accurate partner understanding. Its full human claim remains measurement/data
blocked (MP15).

4. Keep a measurement-first method: independent construct anchors; explicit
domains/units; no same-outcome score validation; equally informed ordinary rivals;
shared-stimulus and measurement-error controls; appropriate held-out people/time;
versioned endpoints; missingness and uncertainty disclosure; and recovery checks
before a new experiment. These are scientific safeguards, not proprietary theory
mechanisms. Reproductions, duplicate documents and repeated simulation seeds do
not become independent empirical replications.

Pruned from the active support claims: a uniquely established decoder mechanism;
the failed K_adapt v0.1 and restricted Lemmens predictive implementations; a
validated full scalar Presence measurement; automatic causal direction from
observational gain; and mathematical necessities/endpoints contradicted by their
own allowed cases. Broader biological and psychological families remain parked
where their exact test was not performed.

Current automatic new-fit queue: empty. Reopening requires the specific evidence
listed in the ledger, not a new label or a convenient replacement proxy.
