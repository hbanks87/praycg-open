# Evidence and claim review

This public copy records the **November 8, 2026** retrospective review of 53 claims across 16 research families. It was prepared for publication on November 10. The review considers existing theory, simulations, public-data analyses and feasibility records. Its status decisions use the November 8 evidence cutoff.

The review found that the examined results did not uniquely establish a framework-specific mechanism. It retains bounded observations and reusable methods, identifies particular implementation failures and sets conditions for reopening unresolved claims.

The action counts are **13 corrections, 26 parked claims, seven retired versions, five limited retentions and two separations**. Each row includes the statement being assessed and the review's decision. These counts describe editorial and research dispositions. They carry no probability that a theory is true.

The retained findings include a same-data human-versus-robot assistance comparison with a bounded older-adult performance and workload result. The model-comparison record includes favorable as well as unfavorable active-inference results, while ordinary alternatives limit claims of a distinctive decoder mechanism. Synthetic identifiability and recovery tests concern their declared generators. Individual calibration observations support instrument feasibility.

The empty automatic new-fit queue in the dated decision describes the public-data searches completed by November 8. Subsequent pilot development and later data reviews have their own records.

The [source inventory](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json) preserves recorded source hashes and archive identities. Its source identifiers name the authoring record and may refer to material retained locally. Use a study's published reproduction package for executable code and its permitted inputs.

The [publication record](PUBLICATION_RECORD.json) documents the public-copy transformations. Computer paths and participant-specific report locations were removed. A participant alias and provisional individual-score detail were generalized. Claim decisions, aggregate metrics and reopening conditions match the original review. Original recordings, participant responses and private feasibility reports remain outside this directory.
