This collection preserves exploratory human pilot outputs, versioned analysis reports, and synthetic reproducibility work. These are different kinds of evidence and should be read with their own source descriptions and limits.

The Contact public folder excludes the copyrighted stimulus video and raw XDF. Its derived outputs help inspect table conventions, visualization, and interpretation. Read the [public data boundary](../ethics-and-claim-boundaries/originals/public_data_boundary.md) before sharing further material.

The Opto-PING bundle retains its scripts, reports, handoff materials, and supporting files together. An external reproduction handoff provides materials for reproduction; its presence alone does not demonstrate independent replication. Synthetic results do not establish a human EEG or cellular mechanism.

All study originals retain their source bytes. Historical paths can be resolved with the [original-file locator](../../docs/reorganization/original-file-index.md); this reorganization does not rerun studies or change their conclusions.
