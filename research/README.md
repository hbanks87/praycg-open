# PRAYCG research

PRAYCG studies how narrative structure and analytic task demands relate to measured brain–body responses. Its record includes proposed explanations, versioned calculations, exploratory human reports and synthetic tests. The [Workbench](../workbench/README.md) provides the software workflow; using it does not require accepting the research theories.

**Navigation reviewed: 25 October 2026.** Scientific documents and evidence retain their own versions and dates.

## Begin with the core documents

1. [Master Theory and Protocol v1.1](theory/originals/docs/PRAYCG_Master_Theory_and_Protocol_PUBLIC_v1_1_v095.md) sets out the research questions and protocol families.
2. [Protocols, Theory and Analysis Methods v0.93–v0.95](protocols/originals/docs/PRAYCG_v0_93_to_v0_95_Protocols_Theory_and_Analysis_Methods.md) supplies versioned definitions, including CAI/SID and prospective study requirements.
3. [Module tier map v1.5.5](measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/MODULE_TIER_MAP_v1_5_5.md) distinguishes proposed primary, secondary and exploratory roles. The [Topo-OSM claim boundary](measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/TOPO_OSM_CLAIM_BOUNDARY_v1_4_7.md) explains the limits of translating human features into biological mechanism claims.

For a concrete evidence trail, follow [Opto-PING](optoping/README.md) from its model and failed specificity tests to the locked [Rung 0O reproduction package](studies/optoping-rung0/Rung0O_LockedNullAwareReproduction/README_RUNG0O.md). The [ladder catalog](ladders/README.md) also identifies earlier documented and prospective stages; a documented rung is not necessarily a completed experiment.

## Read each record by its status

| Record | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Original theory or method specification | The author's stated hypothesis, calculation or proposed procedure at that version. |
| Implementation snapshot | What was found in an identified archive; the [algorithm map](algorithms/README.md) inspects Alpha 11.2.1, not the latest release. |
| Human pilot report | Exploratory analysis of identified recordings and inputs; the Contact self-run is not a confirmatory study. |
| Synthetic reproduction | Behavior under a declared generator, model set, seed policy and decision rule. |
| Roadmap or proposal | Work still planned, including the [research roadmap](roadmap.md). |

Before participant work or sharing outputs, read [safety, ethics and privacy](ethics-and-claim-boundaries/originals/safety_ethics_privacy.md) and the [public data boundary](ethics-and-claim-boundaries/originals/public_data_boundary.md). Definitions, software completion and synthetic success do not by themselves validate a scientific construct.
