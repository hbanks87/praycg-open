# PRAYCG Neuro Research Program

An open research program exploring how naturalistic narratives, sensory structure, and analytic task demands relate to measurable brain–body state changes.

This is the research entrance to PRAYCG. The [Workbench](../workbench/README.md) supports study preparation, acquisition, analysis, and review; the research program contains particular hypotheses, operational definitions, protocols, and exploratory studies. Using the Workbench does not require accepting the program's theories.

## Read the evidence in context

The [Opto-PING hub](optoping/README.md) connects the retained synthetic Rungs 0J–0O to their locked specifications, scripts, reports and reference outputs. The [ladder index](ladders/README.md) also records earlier documented stages and prospective steps without implying that every stage has a runnable package or a completed experiment. The [reproducibility catalog](reproducibility/README.md) distinguishes synthetic replay, exploratory reanalysis and planned replication.

The program asks whether responses to an intact narrative differ from responses to altered stimulus structure and to the same narrative experienced under analytic task demand, after accounting for timing, sensory drive, and artifacts. The [master theory and protocol v1.1](theory/originals/docs/PRAYCG_Master_Theory_and_Protocol_PUBLIC_v1_1_v095.md) contains the full framing and limitations.

| Material | What it contributes | How to read its status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theory documents | Hypotheses and proposed explanations | Document-specific proposals and limits |
| Operational definitions and outputs | Specified quantities and procedures | Computation alone does not validate a construct |
| Human pilot outputs | Examples for methods development | The public Contact self-run is explicitly exploratory |
| Synthetic reproducibility work | Identifiability and specificity tests under stated assumptions | Does not establish a human EEG or cellular mechanism |
| Research roadmap | Proposed controls and study stages | A plan is not a completed study |

Terms such as MRED, A-MRED, KHT-topo, and NUPI refer to definitions and proxies documented by the project. Begin with the [module tier map v1.5.5](measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/MODULE_TIER_MAP_v1_5_5.md) and the [Topo-OSM claim boundary](measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/TOPO_OSM_CLAIM_BOUNDARY_v1_4_7.md) before interpreting a result.

## Preserved research record

The reorganized originals are byte-identical to their source files. Versions, separate occurrences, packaged archives, and companion formats remain separate; a filename containing “Current” describes its historical label. New index pages provide navigation without revising scientific content or assigning a new evidence grade.

Historical documents may still mention their former paths. Use the [original-file locator](../docs/reorganization/original-file-index.md) to find their new locations and the [migration ledger](../docs/reorganization/FILE_MIGRATION_LEDGER.json) to trace each original occurrence.

Before planning participant work or sharing outputs, read [safety, ethics, and privacy](ethics-and-claim-boundaries/originals/safety_ethics_privacy.md) and the [public data boundary](ethics-and-claim-boundaries/originals/public_data_boundary.md). See [citations and attribution](../CITATIONS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md) for sources and [contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for participation.
