# Methods catalog

Use this catalog to follow a proposed quantity from its research question to the original specification, a versioned implementation and the evidence needed to interpret its output. The original notes retain their equations, coefficients and terminology; this page supplies a reading path across them.

**Catalog reviewed: 25 October 2026.** The detailed [implementation map](algorithms/README.md) and its [source manifest](algorithms/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json) describe an inspection of **Public Alpha 11.2.1**. They are not a new audit of Public Alpha 11.9.2. Later Workbench guides are linked separately where they explain newer workflow or attribution changes.

## Signal methods that support interpretation

These methods inspect recordings, references and transformations. Their outputs help assess which measurements support a research calculation.

| Method and guide | Inspected implementation and useful outputs | Interpretation requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gamma Scalpel:** compare measured-electrode gamma before and after declared screening. [v2.0 method guide](../workbench/docs/releases/alpha-10.5.2/Gamma_Scalpel_v2_0/README.md). | [Alpha 11.2.1 implementation description](algorithms/README.md#gamma-scalpel-measured-reference-screening): native windows, measured EMG/EOG reference screening where supported, retained duration and matched condition comparisons. | Preserve electrode identity, units, reference alignment and supported duration. Quiet references do not establish neural origin; measured sites cannot stand in for unrecorded regions. |
| **Optical review:** examine recorded optical changes, with relative hemoglobin conversion only when an instrument profile and recorded data support it. ## What belongs with a reported number

A useful result identifies the exact equation and implementation versions, input recording, channels, units, reference period, event/condition definitions, quality exclusions, available components and missing-data rules. Preserve the component results and failed gates alongside a composite score. Shared input features can create agreement among scores without supplying independent evidence.

For the complete mathematical and historical record, use the [formula/module report v1.0](collections/public-docs/Formula_Module_Report_v1_0/PRAYCG_Formula_Module_Report_v1_0.md), [all method notes and later formula reports](measures/README.md) and [detailed concept guides](concepts/README.md). Cite the actual method version used, with its [bibliography](bibliography/README.md) and [upstream attribution](../CITATIONS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md).
