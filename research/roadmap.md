# Research roadmap

The [Public Alpha 11.2.2 repository update](../docs/updates/2026-10-14-public-workflow-release.md) improves access to the research program while hardening the Workbench. Near-term research priorities remain acquisition evidence, explicit operational definitions, prospective controls where feasible, reproducible analysis and independently inspectable results. Workflow repairs and AI explanations do not replace those milestones. Opto-PING's retained synthetic rungs and participant research remain different evidence categories.

The preserved roadmap describes prospective research direction, including replication, stronger controls, and future endpoints. It is a plan, not evidence that those studies have been completed.
