# Core theory and reading order

The theory record explains PRAYCG's questions, proposed mechanisms and operational definitions. Read the documents below, then follow the method or study relevant to your question. Document versions describe the research record; they are not scientific validation grades.

**Maintained reading guide — reviewed 25 October 2026.** Linked original documents retain their own versions and content.

## Recommended reading

| Order | Open the document | What it contributes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [What this is and is not](../ethics-and-claim-boundaries/originals/what_this_is_and_is_not.md) | A short account of the project's scope and interpretation boundaries. |
| 2 | [Master Theory and Protocol v1.1](originals/docs/PRAYCG_Master_Theory_and_Protocol_PUBLIC_v1_1_v095.md) | The framework: intact narratives, sensory controls, analytic task demands and the protocol families used to compare them. |
| 3 | [Protocols, Theory and Analysis Methods v0.93–v0.95](../protocols/originals/docs/PRAYCG_v0_93_to_v0_95_Protocols_Theory_and_Analysis_Methods.md) | Technical definitions, exploratory analyses and prospective study requirements; separates implementation from proposals. |
| 4 | [Module tier map v1.5.5](../measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/MODULE_TIER_MAP_v1_5_5.md) and [Topo-OSM claim boundary v1.4.7](../measures/originals/master-suite-v1-5-5/TOPO_OSM_CLAIM_BOUNDARY_v1_4_7.md) | Proposed primary, secondary and exploratory roles, and the limits of interpreting human-feature models as cellular mechanisms. |
