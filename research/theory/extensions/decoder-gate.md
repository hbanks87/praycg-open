# Decoder Gate and the reading pilot

Hoyt Banks's Decoder Gate proposal asks how access to a stimulus, prior context and the demands of an intervening task affect what a person understands and retains. PRAYCG has used the name for a proposed explanation, a calculated profile and a reading experiment. A reader interpreting a result needs to know which of these produced it.

| Development | Observable result | What the result can support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decoder Gate Availability (DGA v0.1) in Master Suite v1.5.6 | A branch-level profile combining reported access, availability, emotional impact, task load and confounds. | A description of the available reports and their defined combination. Several terms share ratings. A high profile alone cannot establish an internal gate or successful memory formation. [Original method](../../measures/originals/offline-interpreter-v1-5-6/DGA_Decoder_Gate_Availability_Method_v1_5_6.md) |
| Literary reading pilot, managed v3 | Immediate comprehension and fact responses, arithmetic task checks, delayed facts and optional reading feedback. | Natural Reading describes coherent reading without digits. Comparison crosses coherent/reordered material with narrative/analytic instructions. Reordering can change grammar and difficulty as well as coherence. [Researcher protocol](../../protocols/decoder-gate-pilot/PROTOCOL.md) |
| Independent item review in Public Workbench 11.13 | A material-specific record of which questions a reviewer approved for wording, connected understanding, factual recall or another stated role. | The form records the reviewer, date, declaration of independence, rubric and decision. A reviewer approving a question supplies no participant response and no inter-rater agreement estimate. [Workbench release](https://github.com/hbanks87/praycg-open/releases/tag/PRAYCG_Workbench_Public_A11.13.0) |

## Reading a result

Choose the instrument version, participant, profile, stage, condition and actual follow-up interval. Retain the numbers planned, answered, scored, uncertain and missing. Accuracy uses correct and incorrect scored responses as its denominator. A missing visit supplies no memory score. An item-review file must match the saved material hash. Approved questions can then be summarized by their declared outcome role.

The v3 task does not instantiate every theory prediction. Its arithmetic task cannot qualify a test of surface-word monitoring with preserved wording (C1). V3 has no separate ambiguity-by-prior-context manipulation (C2a) or independently decisive later-evidence revision outcome (C2b). The software marks those endpoints unavailable even after importing an item review. Human agreement, passage difficulty and performance effects each require evidence beyond running the scoring software.

The [pilot guide](../../protocols/decoder-gate-pilot/README.md) identifies the earlier v1 and later v3 instruments. The v3 passages come from works by L. Frank Baum, E. Nesbit, Louisa May Alcott and O. Henry. Banks and PRAYCG selected and adapted materials for the experiment and developed its software. Follow the [source editions and rights record](../../protocols/decoder-gate-pilot/SOURCES_AND_RIGHTS.md) for author credit, the source provider, and material-specific terms.
