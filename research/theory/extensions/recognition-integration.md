# Recognition and later integration

Can someone recognize what an event means in context without showing a new, delayed response? PRAYCG treats these as two questions within the same viewing episode. A familiar scene can remain personally meaningful. Its familiarity also changes what one could reasonably interpret as new encoding. The [Master Theory and Protocol](../originals/docs/PRAYCG_Master_Theory_and_Protocol_PUBLIC_v1_1_v095.md) sets out this distinction.

## How the question developed

On July 28, 2026, Hoyt Banks asked how a viewer moves from seeing or hearing an action, through recognizing its social significance, to understanding what it means in context. A ChatGPT assistant proposed the name *Temporal Semantic Proxy* (TSP) and a scalp comparison using posterior-temporal gamma, frontoparietal gamma, and temporal-jaw and visual comparison signals. The electrode layout allows a comparison among measured scalp signals. It cannot resolve transfer from the superior temporal sulcus to the middle temporal gyrus.

Banks then proposed looking immediately after a local gamma or TSP peak tapers for a possible theta response. This gave the integration question a specific event and time origin. On August 10 he explicitly distinguished familiar, meaningful recognition from possible new encoding. A forecast averaged across viewing times tests a different prediction from a comparison centered on an identified event. Both require the score version, window and timing reference used in that analysis.

| Question | What to examine | Limits on interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did the event evoke recognition-like activity? | The declared TSP and MeaningGamma recipes, their component scalp channels, task-linked activity including TaskGamma, and visual, muscle and artifact controls. | The scores reuse some of the same features. Agreement across scores alone gives no independent evidence of meaning. |
| Did a distinct delayed response follow? | A predeclared anchor or identified local peak, the acute recognition window, and continuous usable data through the delayed theta window. | Retain the recognition result when supported. An unmeasurable delayed window leaves encoding unknown. |
| Did the experience affect later recall or behavior? | An independently scored outcome with its own prompt, delay and source visit. | Scalp activity and later physiological resemblance alone cannot identify what someone learned or remembered. |

The original MRED recipes, endpoint gates and changes in definition are described in the [recognition and integration concept guide](../../concepts/recognition-integration.md). The [11.13 implementation map](../../algorithms/public-alpha-11-13.md) identifies the workers and support required to produce an output.

## Reading the Contact and Field of Dreams pilot records

The inference that the Field of Dreams catch weakened TSP or MeaningGamma was withdrawn. A later scene comparison used archived two-second scores, a provisional timing origin and no closely matched comparison for the primary scenes. It established neither a conflict with the historical XDF analyses nor an independent replication. The participant's account identifies personally meaningful episodes. It supplies neither an exact subjective onset nor an intensity ranking.

To compare a result for the same scene, identify the recording and stimulus edition, clock and time zero, event boundaries, score version and extraction, comparator and controls. Start with the [Contact pilot record](../../studies/contact-pilot/README.md) and the [catalog of versioned pilot reports](../../studies/reports/README.md). Matching a film title alone leaves those differences unresolved.
