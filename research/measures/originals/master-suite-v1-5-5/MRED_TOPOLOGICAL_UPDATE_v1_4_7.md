# MRED Topological Update

**Module:** MRED_v0.2 / Topo-OSM extension

MRED separates **meaning recognition** from **encoding/integration**. The topological update adds a state-persistence term.

## Core distinction

- **MR**: the event was recognized as meaningful, salient, familiar, or schema-relevant.
- **ENC**: the event produced theta-indexed integration, persistence, or topological carryover.
- **TopoPersistence**: measurable change in state topology that persists after the event.

## Updated event model

\[
MR_j=f(MeaningGamma_j,TSP_j,K_{HT-topo,j},SelfReport_j,Artifact_j^{-1})
\]

\[
ENC_j=f(H_{\theta,j},\Delta T_{topo,j},Persistence_j,Novelty_j,Familiarity_j^{-1},Artifact_j^{-1})
\]

where \(\Delta T_{topo,j}\) is the estimated graph/state-topology change following event \(j\).

## Quadrants

| Pattern | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| High MR, high ENC | Meaning recognized and integrated |
| High MR, low ENC | meaning recognition / schema reactivation |
| Low MR, high ENC | nonsemantic encoding, task effect, novelty, or artifact flag |
| Low MR, low ENC | weak or null event |

## Familiarity rule

A familiar scene may produce strong MR without high ENC. This is not necessarily a failure of meaning. It may indicate old-memory reactivation rather than new encoding.

## Claim boundary

MRED does not prove memory formation. It operationalizes candidate recognition/integration dissociations within PR-AYC-G.
