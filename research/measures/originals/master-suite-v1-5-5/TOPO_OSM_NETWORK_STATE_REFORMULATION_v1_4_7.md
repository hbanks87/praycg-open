# Topo-OSM: Network-State Reformulation of Opto-Structural Memory

**Version:** Topo-OSM v0.1

## Rationale

The earlier OSM language included microtubular and biophotonic candidate mechanisms. Those remain speculative lower-scale possibilities, but they are not directly measurable with the current PR-AYC-G stack. The peer-review-facing model therefore adopts a topological formulation.

## Definitions

### Opto

In human PR-AYC-G, **Opto** means externally structured audiovisual input:

\[
u(t) = [luminance, motion, faces, cuts, speech, audio envelope, cue events, scene structure]\]

ALS-PT19 and stimulus fingerprints belong here as physical display and media-timing measurements.

### Structural

**Structural** means network-state topology: the geometry, graph structure, and persistence of measurable brain-body dynamics.

Examples:

- theta topology and carryover;
- lower-gamma work-signal topology;
- TSP/MeaningGamma event geometry;
- alpha suppression / NAST transition structure;
- autonomic availability state;
- persistence across washout;
- condition-specific network reconfiguration.

### Memory

**Memory** means persistence, retrievability, and reactivation of a state-space attractor. It is not defined as survival of one molecule, one synapse, one wave, or one scalp signal.

## Human-scale latent state

The old vulnerable wording was:

\[
Y(t) = Y_{microtubule/biophoton}(t)
\]

The new human-scale wording is:

\[
Y_{topo}(t) = \Phi[A_\theta(t), A_\gamma(t), A_\alpha(t), TSP(t), API_A(t), Resp(t), Artifact(t)]
\]

where:

- \(A_\theta(t)\) = theta-band graph/coherence/carryover topology;
- \(A_\gamma(t)\) = lower-gamma work-signal topology;
- \(A_\alpha(t)\) = alpha/idle-to-absorption topology;
- \(TSP(t)\) = TemporalSemanticProxy;
- \(API_A(t)\) = autonomic availability proxy;
- \(Resp(t)\) = respiratory common-drive covariate;
- \(Artifact(t)\) = artifact and visual-strain burden.

## Human Opto-PING translation

\[
E_{t+\Delta}=a_EE_t+\eta_{HT}Y_{topo,t}+B u_t+\epsilon_E
\]

\[
Y_{topo,t+\Delta}=a_YY_{topo,t}+\zeta_{HT}E_t+C u_t+\epsilon_Y
\]

\[
K_{HT-topo}=\sqrt{|\eta_{HT}\zeta_{HT}|}
\]

Interpretation: \(K_{HT-topo}\) is an event-level coupling proxy between an observed EEG work signal and an inferred topological state proxy. It is not a molecular claim.

## OSM-TOP versus OSM-CELL

| Layer | Meaning | Current measurement status |
|---|---|---|
| OSM-TOP | Human-scale network-state topology | PR-AYC-G can study indirectly |
| OSM-CELL | Cellular/cytoskeletal/biophotonic candidate mechanism | out of scope unless directly measured |

## Updated memory trace split

\[
P(t) = [P_{topo}(t), P_{cell}(t)]
\]

- \(P_{topo}\): macroscopic/mesoscopic topology of circuit-state organization.
- \(P_{cell}\): cellular or cytoskeletal contribution, requiring independent mechanism evidence.

## Peer-review language

Use:

> structured audiovisual input may alter the topology and persistence of measured brain-body dynamics.

Do not use:

> scalp EEG detected microtubule resonance.
