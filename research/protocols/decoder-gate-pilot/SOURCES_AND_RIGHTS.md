## Snapshot and checks

The included `settings.json` is byte-identical to the standalone v3 settings. `PROVENANCE.json` in the ZIP lists the source identities and hashes used for this companion. The November 10 standalone `qa/QUALIFICATION.txt` and local integration `review_dga.md` supply the software-check scope described in the guide. The public ZIP includes no original QA sessions, participant records, runtime logs, book text or scoring keys.

The standalone review checked 38 contiguous excerpts against source editions after whitespace normalization. That supports text fidelity, not independent difficulty matching or scoring validity. The example's output is a known-answer arithmetic demonstration and is labeled synthetic throughout.
