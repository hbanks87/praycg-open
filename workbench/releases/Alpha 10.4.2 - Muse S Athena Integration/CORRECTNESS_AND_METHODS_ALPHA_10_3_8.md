# Alpha 10.3.8 — Correctness and interpretation boundaries

This release preserves PRAYCG's research questions. It neither replaces them with ISC nor validates every proposed construct. Keep software completion, estimability, observed magnitude and justified inference separate.

## Corrected inputs and evidence

Canonical annotation time columns and exact condition aliases replace accidental first-column selection and mismatched names. Exact lock states plus the hash recorded by the runner are required for predeclared anchor use; a filename or the word "unlocked" is not lock evidence. Missing artifact measurements remain unknown. Failed producer tables are not admitted merely because a CSV exists.

Corrected calculations must be saved as new attempts with their implementation identities. Older outputs retain their original meaning and limitations; no original XDF, plan or theory document is rewritten by this build.

## Descriptive effects and uncertainty

The affected exploratory Master contrasts report observed differences rather than sign-only support claims. Overlapping windows are not independent participants or independent replications. Unsupported resampling intervals/p-values are withheld, not displayed as calibrated evidence. Rank pairing within separately standardized conditions does not establish absolute artifact equivalence; it is labeled accordingly. A future confirmatory estimator needs prospectively specified sampling units, dependence handling and multiplicity control.

Micro Handoff's algorithm is not tuned in response to its audit. Its synthetic forward fixture still produces an unfavorable Target–Control contrast, despite approximate lag recovery. Accordingly its construct sensitivity is unvalidated. Development synthetic controls are not held-out human evidence. Brief/sustained/forward/reverse/simultaneous patterns require a broader prospectively frozen temporal validation program before inferential claims.

## Fingerprints and adjustment

Final rendered-media hashes, regressor-file hashes, condition identities and software-observed playback intervals govern the new descriptive alignment route. Physiology windows receive time-weighted fingerprint covariates; raw and pooled standardized versions and exclusions are retained. Unsupported gaps, mismatched media, insufficient windows and rank-deficient designs yield an explicit unavailable model. No row-position join or substitution of a currently selected unrelated stimulus is permitted.

The adjusted/unadjusted outputs use the explicitly recorded model; they do not establish causality, remove all confounds, distinguish muscle from neural gamma, or isolate "meaning." Low-rate visual fingerprints cannot characterize every high-frequency visual change. Per-branch normalization cannot by itself establish matching branch means. Unmeasured higher-order stimulus differences, repeated-exposure effects and collinearity remain limitations.

Phase-scramble/shot-order stimulus generation is not silently changed in this patch. Their low-level differences should be audited with versioned stimulus comparisons; historical rendered media remain historical. First-exposure and deliberate repeated-viewing questions need different interpretation. No universal clip-count requirement is imposed by software.

## Timing and export

Software-flip timestamps improve event bookkeeping but do not measure photon onset, audio onset or clock alignment across devices. Synthetic pulse/noise, receipt coverage and shutdown mocks cannot validate the physical Cerelog five-EMG/two-EOG/ALS system. Continue off-body channel/units/clipping/packet-loss/timestamp testing, then measure inter-board synchronization before artifact-control claims.

BIDS event onset is relative to the first stored task-data point, and regular physiology has no header with columns declared in JSON. These changes follow the [BIDS events specification](https://bids-specification.readthedocs.io/en/stable/modality-agnostic-files/events.html) and [physiology specification](https://bids-specification.readthedocs.io/en/stable/modality-specific-files/physiological-recordings.html), reviewed for version 1.11.1. Independent synthetic readback is narrower than formal whole-dataset validation. Ambiguous clocks and voltage units must not be guessed.

## Explanations, privacy and further work

AI import checks evidence-placeholder substitutions and screens common numerical text forms; it does not establish prose truth, completeness or scientific validity. Human review remains necessary. A failed or unavailable endpoint must not become a null result through its explanation.

Public-oriented derived bundles and optional private full-telemetry bundles remain distinct. Pseudonymous labels do not anonymize XDF headers, markers, dates, device identifiers or stimulus/report content. Original notices/citations and software rights remain in effect.

Deferred, not claimed complete: physical Cerelog validation; cross-device timing; independent-machine verification; complete artifact/transitive environment locking; formal BIDS validation of participant datasets; a broader held-out temporal validation suite; prospective Zuna geometry/seed comparisons; and any live adaptive AI/AUL experiment. The current milestone is dependable evidence for the implemented measurements, not a claim that every neuroscience hypothesis has passed validation.
