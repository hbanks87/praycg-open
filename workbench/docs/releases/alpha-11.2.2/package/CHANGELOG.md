# PRAYCG Workbench - Public - Alpha 11.2.2 — Changelog

This Public edition includes the independent PRAYCG AI Assistant. No private AUL implementation, persistence, prompts, evaluation fixtures or study histories are included. No private extension is needed for public installation, acquisition, analysis, supported protocol authoring or bundle reopening.

## Workflow-hardening patch

- Repairs core/catalog agreement and the Materials dead end.
- Offers recording-aware saved figures without gating every view on an advanced state estimate; keeps paired panes independent and makes failures visible locally.
- Improves numerical evidence projection, source references, scopes and per-module explanations without silently recomputing measurements.
- Separates recording integrity, execution completion, descriptive support, strict endpoints and downstream prerequisites.
- Adds explicit post-hoc recording-context notes, immutable revision provenance, unknown defaults and exact recording bindings.
- Supports optional closed rating contracts in future protocol versions. Existing protocols are unchanged; generic response extraction does not invent scales or comparisons.
- Repairs managed rating credits, malformed-input handling and declared-comparison exports.
- Adds the optional seven-film plus unchanged original Lantern installer: two pinned packages, eight masters, default release URLs, local ZIPs, preflight, safe reuse and Materials handoff.
- Preserves settings, recordings, previous outputs, installed protocols and Zuna assets.

## Retained functionality

- Guided/custom analysis, required QC, persistent module results, dated local summaries and separately authorized AI explanations.
- Scoped Athena reports with explicit channel/pair/all-four and accepted/all-candidate/full-interval views.
- Signal Quality & Cleaning Review, actual pinned NeuroKit2 and mne-denoise comparisons, original/candidate/removed views, discontinuity and adjacent-interval handling, and versioned exclusions.
- Atlas recording-versus-endpoint compatibility, exploratory Athena recording policy, operational identity/ownership checks, XDF replay and passive live views.
- Local snapshots, report history, source-bound explanatory derivatives, Methods and Credits, and reviewed research bundles.
