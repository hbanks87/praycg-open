# Public Alpha 11.4.1 attribution and citation supplement

**Reviewed 18 October 2026.** This supplement records the optical-credit correction and the current software relationships in Public Alpha 11.4.1. It supplements the [master attribution record](../CITATIONS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md), whose earlier review remains historical. It is a focused source and citation review, not a complete historical provenance audit or evidence that every listed method ran in a study.

## Optical-credit correction

The current registry's `optical_verified_relative_hemoglobin` method previously contained a reference with only a URL. It lacked the citation identifier and readable citation required by automatic Methods and Credits. Alpha 11.4.1 supplies a complete original-method reference and retains the MNE-Python tutorial as separately identified context. The release also checks that current method references have usable identifiers, citations and URLs.

**Original method reference:** Delpy, D. T.; Cope, M.; van der Zee, P.; Arridge, S.; Wray, S.; Wyatt, J. (1988). *Estimation of optical pathlength through tissue from direct time of flight measurement.* Physics in Medicine and Biology, 33(12), 1433–1442. [DOI: 10.1088/0031-9155/33/12/008](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9155/33/12/008). [Original article metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3237772/). All six authors are retained.

**Retained documentation reference:** MNE-Python contributors. *Preprocessing functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) data.* MNE-Python documentation. [Official tutorial](https://mne.tools/stable/auto_tutorials/preprocessing/70_fnirs_processing.html), accessed 18 October 2026. The tutorial is methodological context. It does not establish that PRAYCG's optical worker executes MNE's `optical_density` or `beer_lambert_law` functions.

PRAYCG's optical worker implements its own profile-dependent, natural-log optical-density and modified Beer–Lambert inversion using declared wavelength pairing, extinction coefficients, source–detector distances and pathlength factors. The instrument profile and source binding remain necessary. This correction changes attribution metadata; it does not change the numerical optical calculation, establish a verified Athena wavelength mapping, or validate physiological interpretation. The bundled Athena profile remains available for raw review while relative hemoglobin conversion requires independently supported instrument information. Relative HbO/HbR changes do not establish absolute oxygenation, cortical origin or a clinical endpoint.

The current runtime registry is `config/attribution_registry_v1.json`. The older files under `attribution/` retain their original review scope and are not substituted for the current registry. Historical recordings, analysis outputs and released archives retain their original evidence; new credits do not retrospectively prove that an old run executed a method.

Earlier Alpha 11.2.1/11.2.2 statements that no ASR or ICLabel integration was identified describe those historical packages. They do not describe the routes now present. Likewise, PRAYCG's original first-harmonic respiration/RR coupling remains a distinct measure, while the current worker also offers a separately identified NeuroKit2 RSA comparison. The two are not interchangeable estimators.

## Software and original-method references

**S1 — NeuroKit2.** Makowski, Dominique; Pham, Tam; Lau, Zen J.; Brammer, Jan C.; Lespinasse, François; Pham, Hung; Schölzel, Christopher; Chen, S. H. Annabel (2021). *NeuroKit2: A Python toolbox for neurophysiological signal processing.* Behavior Research Methods, 53(4), 1689–1696. [DOI: 10.3758/s13428-020-01516-y](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-020-01516-y). [Official citation instructions and full author list](https://neuropsychology.github.io/NeuroKit/cite_us.html). Record the actual installed version; the inspected requirement is 0.2.13.

The ECG comparison selects the `neurokit` and `pantompkins1985` routes. The latter retains Pan, J.; Tompkins, W. J. (1985), *A Real-Time QRS Detection Algorithm*, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, BME-32(3), 230–236, [DOI: 10.1109/TBME.1985.325532](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.1985.325532). Correction candidates use `signal_fixpeaks(method="Kubios")`, retaining Lipponen, J. A.; Tarvainen, M. P. (2019), *A robust algorithm for heart rate variability time series artefact correction using novel beat classification*, Journal of Medical Engineering & Technology, 43(3), 173–181, [DOI: 10.1080/03091902.2019.1640306](https://doi.org/10.1080/03091902.2019.1640306). This is a NeuroKit implementation and does not imply a Kubios binary is shipped.

The respiration reference configures `khodadad2018` and `harrison2021`; PPG configures `elgendi` with `templatematch` quality; EDA uses the `neurokit` processing route. The optional `hrv_rsa` comparison uses measured paired ECG and respiration, and the frequency comparison calls `hrv_frequency(psd_method="welch")` within supported intact interval runs. Keep the selected API's original method references and recorded settings alongside the software citation: [ECG](https://neuropsychology.github.io/NeuroKit/functions/ecg.html), [respiration](https://neuropsychology.github.io/NeuroKit/functions/rsp.html), [PPG](https://neuropsychology.github.io/NeuroKit/functions/ppg.html), [EDA](https://neuropsychology.github.io/NeuroKit/functions/eda.html), [HRV/RSA](https://neuropsychology.github.io/NeuroKit/functions/hrv.html). PPG-derived pulse variability is not ECG HRV, template similarity is not a ground-truth certificate, and the EDA route is not cvxEDA. The retained [Signal Review 11.2.0 supplement](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES/SIGNAL_REVIEW_11_2_0.md) and its original notices retain their earlier scope.

## Notices and scope

The ZIP contains PRAYCG adapters, documentation, method records and retained upstream notices. NeuroKit2, ASRpy, MNE-ICALabel, Pycrostates and related implementation distributions are installed separately; their inclusion in requirements does not transfer their authorship or licenses to PRAYCG. The optional Signal Extras installer records actual installed versions and discovered notice hashes in its release-local environment. The retained [EEG extensions notice](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES/EEG_EXTENSIONS_11_3_0.txt) includes exact inspected ASRpy, MNE-ICALabel and Pycrostates license texts. The [third-party notices index](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES/README.md) remains the entry point for other preserved notices.

This supplement does not refresh every historical source hash, establish complete transitive/native-license coverage, or validate a scientific inference. Citations credit contributions; software checks establish only their stated scope. PRAYCG-specific integration, support rules and review policies remain separately identified, and study-level reports should credit only the methods they actually used.
