PRAYCG EEG review extensions: upstream software and methods
Attribution record revision: PRAYCG_EEGExtensions_11.3.0_v1

Distribution boundary
The Workbench archive contains PRAYCG adapters, tests and method records, not
ASRpy, ICLabel model files, Pycrostates, ONNX binaries or copied upstream solver
source. The explicit optional installer downloads them into a separate release-
local environment. Their original upstream notices remain in their installed
distributions. PRAYCG's license cannot relicense these upstream components.

Direct optional requirements are pinned in requirements_signal_extras.txt.
The installer records observed installed versions and hashes of discovered
license/notice files, including transitive distributions, in
.signal-review-env/UPSTREAM_INSTALLED_NOTICES.json. NumPy/SciPy wheels include
their own numerical-library notices. This inventory is not a complete audit of
every native object, historical source similarity or patent rights.

New adapters call actual upstream APIs; they do not claim upstream endorsement:
MNE 1.11.0: https://mne.tools/1.11/ (BSD-3-Clause)
scikit-learn 1.9.0: https://scikit-learn.org/ (BSD-3-Clause)
ASRpy 0.0.8: https://github.com/DiGyt/asrpy (BSD-3-Clause)
MNE-ICALabel 0.10.0: https://github.com/mne-tools/mne-icalabel (BSD-3-Clause)
Pycrostates 0.6.1: https://github.com/vferat/pycrostates (BSD-3-Clause)
ONNX Runtime 1.31.0: https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime (MIT; retain its
own bundled third-party notices when redistributing an installed environment)
NumPy 1.26.4 / SciPy 1.17.1: https://numpy.org/ / https://scipy.org/
EEGLABIO 0.1.2 / EDFIO 0.4.10 / pybv 0.7.6 supply optional test/export support;
their upstream notices remain with the downloaded distributions.

Actual operational definitions and method references are in
tools/Signal_Review_v1_0/methods_eeg_extensions_v1.json. Results attach only the
method record they actually ran. Method citations do not replace licenses.
ASRpy's documentation cites a patent application; the software license alone
does not establish comprehensive patent clearance. This is not legal advice.
No code from iCanClean, EEGPrep, AMICA, SOUND or SPAR benchmarks is included in
these EEG extension files. The centered reference-regression candidate is a
PRAYCG-defined linear model, not iCanClean. EEGPrep remains explicitly deferred.

The following exact notices are reproduced from the inspected installed wheels
as a convenience. This does not substitute for inspecting all notices if an
environment or additional upstream binaries are redistributed.
