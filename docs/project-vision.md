# PRAYCG project vision

PRAYCG is an open, local-first neuroscience ecosystem intended to guide a study from initial concept through reproducible publication.

The Workbench connects workspace creation, protocol selection and stimulus fingerprinting with hardware configuration, synchronized acquisition, protocol execution, analysis, visualization and offline interpretation. Its organizing principle is to preserve the identities and decisions needed for someone else to inspect what happened.

The Neuro Research Program develops particular hypotheses, protocols and interpretations. It remains a distinct body of work that can be challenged, extended or rejected without making the software unusable for other research.

## A shared technical language

The intended audience spans institutional laboratories, private companies, independent researchers and curious individuals. Common formats and explicit provenance should make assumptions, methods, timing, limitations and results easier to evaluate across settings.

Standardization does not make an experiment scientifically valid. A finished workflow can still contain weak hypotheses, confounds, insufficient data, unreliable measurements or unsupported interpretations.

## Reproducible exchange

The long-term aim is privacy-reviewed research bundles containing appropriate data, protocol and stimulus identities, acquisition evidence, analysis code, software descriptions, reports and reproducible visualizations.

A research catalog can help others inspect a study, independently analyze its data or attempt replication. Publication still requires consent, rights and human privacy review. A private full-study or AI-review package is not automatically suitable for public release.

## Development principles

- Preserve original evidence and distinguish later reanalysis.
- Keep software execution, data quality, estimability and scientific interpretation separate.
- Make speculative or exploratory work identifiable without erasing it.
- Prefer tested, bounded integrations with visible limitations.
- Preserve theory history while improving the clarity of current navigation.
