# Safety, ethics, and privacy

- Obtain informed consent for any human participant recording.
- Do not conceal the general nature of physiological recording.
- Do not publish raw biometric data from others without explicit written permission.
- Avoid uploading raw XDF files publicly unless they are fully deidentified and ethically cleared.
- Do not use PRAYCG to diagnose, rank, shame, or manipulate people.
- Emotional stimuli can be distressing; screen and debrief appropriately.
- Respect copyrighted media. Use public-domain, CC-licensed, or personally created media for public demos.
