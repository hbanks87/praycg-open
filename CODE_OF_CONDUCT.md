# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project discusses human meaning, physiology, speculative mechanisms, and public science. The work is only useful if disagreement remains precise, humane, and safe.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- be respectful and direct;
- criticize claims, code, methods, and interpretations rather than people;
- preserve the project boundaries and avoid inflated claims;
- welcome careful falsification and negative results;
- protect participant privacy and safety;
- disclose conflicts of interest when relevant;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, doxxing, or hostile personal conduct.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- personal attacks or harassment;
- sharing private participant data or private communications without permission;
- presenting exploratory findings as clinical, diagnostic, therapeutic, or proven mechanism claims;
- unsafe hardware or electrical advice;
- intentionally misleading documentation;
- uploading copyrighted media as if it were freely redistributable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainer using a private channel listed in the public repository profile or issue tracker. If a concern involves immediate safety, follow local emergency procedures first.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior undermines safety, privacy, reproducibility, or respectful collaboration.
