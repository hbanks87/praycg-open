# Firmware provenance and redistribution status

The compact PRAYCG sketch descends from the five-EMG differential adaptation of Cerelog Inc.’s [V1 special differential firmware](https://github.com/Cerelog-ESP-EEG/ESP-EEG/blob/af1a56e0127e71606afdc7e7ddf0ca831715e09c/firmware/other/V1_special_differential_fw/V1_special_differential_fw.ino), upstream commit `af1a56e0127e71606afdc7e7ddf0ca831715e09c`. The compact revision disables CH1 and CH2, changes the firmware identity and profile metadata, and requires the eight-channel ADS1299 identity at register readback. [VERIFICATION.json](VERIFICATION.json) identifies the local sketch and compiled application by checksum; neither file is distributed here.

The [upstream license at that commit](https://github.com/Cerelog-ESP-EEG/ESP-EEG/blob/af1a56e0127e71606afdc7e7ddf0ca831715e09c/LICENSE) names **Copyright (c) 2025 Cerelog Inc.** and labels the firmware directory MIT, but the supplied text ends its firmware terms with the literal placeholder `[Standard MIT License Text Continues...]`. The [preserved license text](../2%20EOG%20%2B%205%20EMG%20%2B%20ALS-PT19/licenses/CERELOG_UPSTREAM_LICENSE.txt) matches the upstream file. The [earlier redistribution review](../2%20EOG%20%2B%205%20EMG%20%2B%20ALS-PT19/LICENSE_NOTICE.md) also records the Arduino ESP32 and ESP-IDF runtime notices relevant to a compiled distribution.

The compact sketch and image remain local pending confirmation of the complete intended firmware terms and review of the applicable runtime notices and corresponding-source requirements for that build. The public profile, checksum and verification records do not grant a license to those local files.
